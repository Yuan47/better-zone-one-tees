# Separate Dannon pictures and customer sign-in branding

The adult and kids Round Neck and V-Neck listings each have a single, correctly shaped sample shirt in all 14 existing colors. The 56 controlled SVG files are generated by `scripts/generate-dannon-previews.cjs` using the store's existing illustration geometry and approximate palette. They are sample illustrations, not manufacturer photographs or confirmed physical swatches.

`DannonProductImages` recognizes only the 28 original combined-neckline sample filenames on the four existing Dannon product slugs. Product and variant photo presentation resolves those samples to the appropriate new picture. Database image references are preserved. Owner-uploaded photos, external product photo URLs, blank-variant fallback and other brands keep their usual behavior. Known product context is reused when presenting all variants so image selection does not add a query for every size/color.

Customer account screens display the supplied Zone One Tee's logo. Customer sign-in, registration and public password-recovery screens no longer advertise a staff/owner sign-in link. Staff and owners still use `/admin/login`; its authentication, role checks, verification, owner-created staff setup and customer-only Google sign-in rules are unchanged. Removing the public cross-link is a navigation change, not an authorization boundary.

This revision needs no data migration and does not change accounts, prices, stock, invoices, payments or refunds. Focused combined verification passed 192 tests / 2,636 assertions on 3 November 2026 with isolated data and simulated/disabled external providers. Hosted publication and visual acceptance are recorded separately by the deployment operator.
