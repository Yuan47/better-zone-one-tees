# Lalamove delivery demonstration

Use PayMongo TEST payments and Lalamove Sandbox throughout this demonstration. No real money or physical delivery is involved.

1. Check the approved store pickup entrance and demonstration phone number. Sign in to the Philippines Lalamove Partner Portal, choose Sandbox and check its virtual credit.
2. Create a TEST customer order. Enter an 11-digit mobile number beginning with 09, a complete delivery address and a confirmed map pin at the delivery entrance. Request the fee and review clothing plus delivery before paying in full through the test simulator.
3. Staff open the provider-confirmed paid order, check sizes/colors/quantities, pack it and record a reason when marking Ready for delivery.
4. Choose Book Lalamove sandbox delivery once. If the fee increases, the owner can approve a maximum total courier cost and cover the difference. The customer's invoice stays unchanged.
5. In the same Sandbox booking, use the available test-driver controls to simulate rider progress. Back in the store order, Check delivery with Lalamove reads the provider's saved status.
6. An uncertain booking must be reconciled or recovered before another booking. A cancelled booking can be rebooked only after verification. Preserve its original invoice and booking history.
7. After the provider confirms completion, the customer selects one to five stars and confirms receipt. The written review is optional. An online order stays To Ship until the customer confirms; staff cannot submit that confirmation for the customer.

The store shows the fee in checkout and in the saved invoice. The map pin determines the quotation destination. A saved address or GPS shortcut does not replace checking the actual entrance. Provider quotations can expire or change, so request a fresh fee after changing the address or pin.

The same short guide is available in the approved Staff FAQ library and as a collapsed note on Sandbox Lalamove order pages. Customers cannot obtain internal staff guides through customer chat.
