## Corrections included in the final checks

- Invalid or expired delivery quotations are cleared before payment creation or stock reservation. Address changes require a fresh quotation. PayMongo financial responses and Lalamove quotation, booking and tracking responses disable redirects and require HTTP2xx before their data can be accepted. Existing HTTP4xx/5xx handling, including the definite insufficient-credit recovery, is retained. The final Lalamove subset passed 166 tests / 1,287 assertions.
- An unsupported AI fabric claim found in browser testing was removed from the answer path. Recommendations and product facts use verified catalog names, offered variants and available stock; unknown fabric or comfort details require staff confirmation. Approved FAQs and commerce rules retain priority.
- Customer guides now include full name, unique username, Google customer sign-in and missing receipt-email help. The additive changes preserve owner-edited or inactive answers. Missing receipt guidance explicitly tells the customer to use the original invoice and never pay again to obtain an email.
- Store receipt emails run separately from financial and webhook transactions. Each verified installment has its own durable notification claim, receipt reference, amount and balance after that payment. Interrupted or uncertain sends are blocked from automatic resend. Brevo's duplicate-idempotency response remains uncertain in receipts, readiness messages and reminders; it does not authorize a staff retry. The focused readiness/reminder/FAQ subset passed 97 tests / 686 assertions before the final full regression run.
- Deployment verification preserved original records and retained private backups. No actual main-store purchase was fabricated to claim acceptance, and no production payment or courier booking was used.
